Tips for Reaching Your Goals

Weight Loss

  • Create a moderate calorie deficit of 500-750 calories per day
  • Focus on protein intake to preserve muscle mass
  • Include both cardio and strength training in your routine

Muscle Gain

  • Consume a slight calorie surplus of 250-500 calories daily
  • Aim for 1.6-2.2g of protein per kg of bodyweight
  • Focus on progressive overload in resistance training

General Health

  • Stay hydrated with 2-3 liters of water daily
  • Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep per night
  • Include a variety of fruits and vegetables for micronutrients
